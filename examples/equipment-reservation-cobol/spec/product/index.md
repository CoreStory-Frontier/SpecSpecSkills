# Product

* [Product overview](overview.md) - Equipment Reservation product intent and scope.

## Capabilities

* [Find equipment](capabilities/find-equipment.md) - Employees find equipment that is available for a selected period.
* [Reserve equipment](capabilities/reserve-equipment.md) - Employees reserve an available equipment item without conflicts.
* [View reservations](capabilities/view-reservations.md) - Employees view only their own equipment reservations.
* [Manage equipment availability](capabilities/manage-equipment-availability.md) - Custodians manage equipment and maintenance periods.
* [Track equipment use](capabilities/track-equipment-use.md) - Custodians record pickup, return, and equipment-use history.
* [Control employee access](capabilities/control-employee-access.md) - The product controls employee access to equipment functions.
