## Approved identity substitute

`EQP_ACTR` contains ALICE, BOB, CASEY, and DREW. This local role table replaces the external workforce identity provider for the reference only. It does not prove real workforce integration.

## Approved behavior decisions

1. Periods are half-open and UTC-normalized. A booking ending at 11:00 does not conflict with one starting at 11:00.
2. Only confirmed Reservations block availability.
3. Maintenance invalidates overlapping confirmed Reservations only.
4. Return is permitted after invalidation or cancellation and preserves that Reservation state. A return completes a still-confirmed Reservation.
