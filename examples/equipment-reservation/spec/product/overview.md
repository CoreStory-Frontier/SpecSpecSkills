# Summary

Equipment Reservation helps employees find, reserve, and review shared workplace equipment.
Custodians manage equipment availability, handoffs, returns, and equipment-use history.

# Users

- Employee: Finds and reserves available equipment and views their own Reservations.
- Custodian: Manages equipment, maintenance periods, pickup, return, and invalid reservations.

# Capabilities

- [Find equipment](capabilities/find-equipment.md) - Employees find equipment that is available for a selected period.
- [Reserve equipment](capabilities/reserve-equipment.md) - Employees reserve an available equipment item without conflicts.
- [View reservations](capabilities/view-reservations.md) - Employees view only their own equipment reservations.
- [Manage equipment availability](capabilities/manage-equipment-availability.md) - Custodians manage equipment and maintenance periods.
- [Track equipment use](capabilities/track-equipment-use.md) - Custodians record pickup, return, and equipment-use history.
- [Control employee access](capabilities/control-employee-access.md) - The product controls employee access to equipment functions.

# Goals

- Let employees find available equipment.
- Let employees reserve one physical equipment item for a defined period.
- Let employees view their own reservations.
- Prevent overlapping reservations for the same equipment item.
- Let custodians manage equipment and maintenance periods.
- Let custodians record pickup and return.
- Keep a traceable history of equipment use.
- Control employee access to equipment functions.

# Non-goals

- Purchase equipment.
- Process payments.
- Manage employee identities.
- Control physical access to equipment automatically.

# Success metrics

- Fewer conflicting equipment reservations.
- Equipment availability that matches reservations and maintenance periods.
- A traceable history for equipment pickup, return, and use.
